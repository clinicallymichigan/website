What we do

Programs built for curious classrooms.

We design every Clinically session to fit into a single class period — 45 to 60 minutes of focused, honest, and interactive conversation about what it really takes to enter medicine.

In-School Presentations

Our flagship program. A trained Clinically presenter visits your school and delivers a tailored, interactive talk that demystifies the premed pathway — from high school course selection to med school applications.

  • 45–60 min
  • Grades 9–12
  • Free for schools

Mentorship Match

After a presentation, interested students are paired with current premed undergraduates for ongoing 1-on-1 mentorship — answering questions, reviewing plans, and providing real talk throughout the year.

  • 1-on-1
  • Monthly check-ins
  • Virtual

Resource Library

A growing, free library of guides, timelines, and printable planning tools — covering everything from high school course selection to summer programs, shadowing etiquette, and MCAT prep timelines.

  • Always free
  • PDF + web
  • Updated yearly

Med Student Panels

Periodic virtual panels featuring current medical students from a range of schools and specialties. Students submit questions in advance and hear honest, lived-experience answers from people who just walked the path.

  • Quarterly
  • Virtual
  • Q&A format
Why it matters

The premed path is opaque. We're making it readable.

Most high school students interested in medicine have no access to premed advising. The result? Talented students — especially those from underrepresented backgrounds — walk away from the path before they even start.

No Access

Most high schools offer no premed or health-career advising. Students are left to figure out a complex, high-stakes path entirely on their own — or not at all.

No Mentorship

Without guidance, talented students — especially those who are first-generation — often abandon the pathway before they even start college, due to lack of information, not lack of ability.

No Roadmap

The medical school pathway is opaque. Requirements, timelines, and exams can feel impossible to navigate without a clear, step-by-step map laid out by someone who has done it.

"
I thought you had to be rich, or know a doctor, to even consider medical school. Clinically was the first time someone told me, step by step, how a kid like me could actually get there. Two years later, I'm a biology major prepping for the MCAT.
Maya R. Pilot program student · now premed undergrad
Presentation topics

Seven sessions. One roadmap.

Schools can book a single session, mix and match, or host the full series across a semester. Every talk is interactive, jargon-free, and built for a class period.

01 / Roadmap

The Premed Pathway

Year-by-year breakdown of what to do in high school — courses, grades, extracurriculars, and the milestones that actually matter for college premed readiness.

45 minutes
02 / Test

MCAT Demystified

What the MCAT actually is, when to start preparing, how it's scored, and how to think about it without panic. Includes a sample question walkthrough.

50 minutes
03 / Experience

Beyond the Books

Clinical hours, shadowing, research, and volunteering — what each one means, how much you actually need, and how to find opportunities as a high schooler.

45 minutes
04 / Admissions

Inside Med School Admissions

What admissions committees actually look at — and what they don't. A transparent look at GPA, MCAT, personal statements, interviews, and holistic review.

60 minutes
05 / Careers

Specialties Spotlight

From pediatrics to pathology to surgery — a tour of medical specialties, what daily life looks like in each, and how students start exploring fit early.

45 minutes
06 / Reality

The Premed Reality Check

Finances, time commitment, burnout, and joy. An honest conversation about the cost of medical school, debt, length of training, and why people still choose it.

60 minutes
07 / Stories

Q&A with Med Students

A rotating panel of current medical students answering submitted questions — covering doubts, mistakes, surprises, and the advice they wish they'd gotten earlier.

60 minutes
08 / Custom

Build Your Own

Don't see what you need? We work with teachers and counselors to design custom sessions — career day talks, parent nights, or specialized workshops for health occupations classes.

How it works

Booking is simple. Four steps.

From first email to presentation day, the process takes about two weeks. We handle content, materials, and presenter coordination — you just provide the classroom.

01

Reach Out

Submit the booking form below with your school, audience, and preferred dates. Takes less than three minutes.

02

Tailor Together

We schedule a 15-minute call to understand your students' needs and pick the right topics and presenter.

03

We Present

Our presenter arrives at your school (or joins virtually) and delivers the session — interactive, real, and on time.

04

Follow-Up

Students leave with a printed roadmap, access to our resource library, and an invitation to mentorship.
